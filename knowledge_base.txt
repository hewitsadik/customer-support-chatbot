CUSTOMER SUPPORT CHATBOT – KNOWLEDGE BASE

Purpose:
This chatbot provides basic customer support for a fictional online store.

1. STORE HOURS
Keywords: hours, open, close, closed
Response:
We're open Monday-Friday from 9 AM to 6 PM and Saturday from 10 AM to 4 PM. We are closed on Sunday.

2. RETURNS AND REFUNDS
Keywords: return, refund
Response:
You can return eligible items within 30 days of purchase with the original receipt. Refunds are issued to the original payment method.

3. SHIPPING
Keywords: shipping, ship
Response:
We offer free standard shipping on orders of $50 or more. Standard shipping usually takes 3-5 business days.

4. PAYMENT METHODS
Keywords: payment, pay
Response:
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, and PayPal.

5. ORDER TRACKING
Keywords: track, order
Response:
You can track your order using the tracking link in your shipping confirmation email.

6. CONTACT INFORMATION
Keywords: contact, email
Response:
You can contact customer support Monday-Friday from 9 AM to 6 PM by email at support@example.com.

7. GREETINGS
Examples: hello, hi, hey
Response:
Hello! How can I help you today?

8. THANK-YOU MESSAGES
Keywords: thank, thanks
Response:
You're welcome! I'm happy to help.

9. HELP COMMAND
Command: help
Response:
The chatbot displays the topics it can answer, including store hours, returns and refunds, shipping, payment methods, order tracking, and contact information.

10. FALLBACK RESPONSE
If the chatbot does not recognize the question:
I'm sorry, I don't have information about that yet. Type 'help' to see the topics I can answer.
